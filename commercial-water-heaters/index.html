Warning Signs to Watch For

Most tanks announce trouble weeks or months ahead with rumbling noises, rusty water, or hot showers that keep getting shorter. If any of the following sound familiar, it's worth having the unit checked:

  • Higher utility bills that point to a heater losing efficiency
  • Dish or hand sink temperatures drifting below what your inspector expects
  • A recirculation loop that no longer seems to do its job
  • Rumbling, popping, or banging from a heavily used tank
  • Rusty water or damp spots around the equipment
  • A heater old enough that a failure feels like a matter of time
  • Scalding bursts or wild temperature swings at showers or sinks
  • Hot water running out during your busiest hour

Prevention Tips That Actually Help

A little routine care goes a long way toward keeping a water heater running well past its expected lifespan. Here are our recommendations:

  • Keep the area around the heaters clear for combustion air and service access
  • Keep recirculation pumps and check valves in service to avoid long waits and wasted energy
  • Test thermostatic mixing valves periodically to confirm safe delivery temperatures
  • Flush commercial tanks more often than residential ones, often every few months in heavy use or hard water
  • Replace consumed anode rods before the tank itself starts to corrode
  • Record fixture temperatures regularly, especially before inspections
  • Plan replacement before an aging unit fails during peak season
