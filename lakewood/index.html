Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Lakewood

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, lift the T&P valve lever annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which limits scalding risk and cuts down on scale. Tankless owners should plan on a descaling flush once a year in hard-water areas.

The age of your heater plays a big role. With regular service, a tank water heater usually runs 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rust-colored water, popping noises, or moisture around the base, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new heater with water spreading across the floor.

Left alone, a small problem tends to grow. Take a spent anode rod: swapping it out is a quick, affordable repair, but once it's gone, the water begins corroding the tank steel, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a surprise cleanup.

Typical Water Heater Issues in Lakewood

Whatever type of water heater your Lakewood home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tank heaters usually give trouble through pilot assemblies and thermocouples, dirty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric units burn out heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless heaters show error codes for scale buildup, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
