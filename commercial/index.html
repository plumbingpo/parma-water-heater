Kitchen Hot Water

Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and an undersized or failing heater shows up first as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and a backed-up dish room. A high-temperature dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater paired with the main system. Our food-service work covers high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Staff can't run the dish room without hot water that reaches the right temperature, and health inspectors notice lukewarm handwashing sinks right away. Our service keeps temperatures and recovery where your kitchen needs them, backed by service logs and written reports after each visit. That paperwork shows a well-maintained facility to inspectors and landlords.

We work around your business, not the other way around. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and we keep downtime to a minimum so the kitchen opens on time.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect venting and draft, confirm proper combustion air, test relief valves and their discharge lines, verify expansion tanks are charged correctly, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Consistent professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Parma Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Parma and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening to limit downtime.

Once it's running, we set up a maintenance schedule to prevent a repeat, and each response comes with full documentation for your records.
